F&B Industry

WSH support built for kitchens & restaurants

F&B is one of Singapore’s highest-risk sectors for workplace injuries. We know the hazards, the MOM requirements, and how to keep your kitchen compliant without disrupting service.

Key hazards in your industry

These are the most common risk areas that MOM inspectors focus on — and where incidents most frequently occur.

Burns & scalds

Deep fryers, hot surfaces, and steam equipment are the most common sources of serious injury in F&B. Your RA must address every heat source.

Slip & fall

Wet kitchen floors cause more incidents than any other hazard in F&B. Anti-slip mats, drainage, and wet-floor protocols must be documented.

LPG & gas safety

Gas leaks and improper hose connections are a leading cause of fires. Hose condition, regulator checks, and emergency isolation procedures must be in place.

Manual handling

Carrying heavy stock, repetitive chopping, and awkward postures cause cumulative injuries. Most are preventable with simple SWPs.

Chemical handling

Cleaning agents, degreasers, and sanitisers require SDS sheets, proper PPE, and segregation from food storage areas.

Fire & evacuation

Grease fires require Class K extinguishers. Exhaust hoods must be cleaned on schedule. Fire exits must be unobstructed at all times.
