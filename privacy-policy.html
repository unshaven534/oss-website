Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026  —  Overwatch Strategic Solutions  —  UEN 201534119G

Overwatch Strategic Solutions ("OSS", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use and protect it — in plain language, not legalese.

We comply with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

1. What data we collect

We collect personal data only when you voluntarily provide it to us — for example when you fill in a contact form, request a free audit, or sign up as a referral partner. This may include:

  • Your name and job title
  • Business name and address
  • Email address and phone number
  • Details about your business you share with us (number of staff, industry sector, WSH concerns)

We do not collect sensitive personal data (e.g. NRIC numbers, financial account details) through this website.

2. How we use your data

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or consultation request
  • Provide the WSH services you have engaged us for
  • Send you relevant safety resources or updates (only if you have opted in)
  • Process referral commissions where applicable
  • Comply with our legal obligations as a MOM-registered WSHO

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Ever.

3. Who we share data with

We share personal data only where necessary to deliver our services:

  • Formspree — our form submission processor (servers in the US; data handled under their privacy policy)
  • Ministry of Manpower (MOM) — where required by law (e.g. incident notifications, WSHO appointment records)
  • Service providers — accountants or legal advisors, bound by confidentiality

All third-party processors we use are required to protect your data to a standard consistent with the PDPA.

4. How long we keep your data

We retain personal data for as long as it is needed to fulfil the purpose it was collected for, or as required by law. Enquiry data from non-clients is deleted within 12 months if no engagement follows. Client engagement records are retained for a minimum of 5 years in line with MOM WSH documentation requirements.

5. Cookies and website analytics

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. If this changes, this policy will be updated and visitors will be notified via a cookie banner before any data is collected.

6. Your rights under the PDPA

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct any inaccurate data
  • Withdrawal of consent — opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Data portability — request your data in a machine-readable format where technically feasible

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. Our website is served over HTTPS. Form submissions are encrypted in transit.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Material changes will be communicated via our website.
