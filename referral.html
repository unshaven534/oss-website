Key terms

Simple rules, no hidden catches.

3-month qualifying period

The referred client must complete 3 consecutive months on a paid OSS subscription before the reward is released.

One-time payout

Your reward is paid in one transfer within 14 working days of the 3-month mark. No ongoing admin, no monthly tracking.

Early exit — no reward

If the client cancels before completing 3 months, no reward is paid. The full 3-month period must be fulfilled.

No limit on referrals

You can refer as many businesses as you like. Each qualifying referral earns its own separate reward.

New clients only

The referral programme applies to businesses that are not already OSS clients at the time of the referral.

Any industry welcome

We work with F&B, workshops, warehouses, retail, and offices. If they need WSH support, we can help.
