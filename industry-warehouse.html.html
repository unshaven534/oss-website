    WSH support built for warehouses & logistics

    Key hazards in your industry

    These are the most common risk areas that MOM inspectors focus on — and where incidents most frequently occur.

    Forklift & MHE safety

    Forklifts are involved in more fatal incidents than almost any other piece of equipment. Operator certification, pre-use checks, and pedestrian segregation must all be documented.

    Racking & storage

    Overloaded or damaged racking is a collapse waiting to happen. Load capacity must be displayed, and inspections must be logged.

    Manual handling

    Repetitive lifting, awkward postures, and high-frequency tasks cause most of the non-acute injuries in warehousing. SWPs and training records are required.

    Working at height

    Mezzanines, tall racking, and loading docks require fall protection, guardrails, and documented procedures for elevated work.

    Slip, trip & fall

    Loading dock edges, floor debris, and poor lighting are persistent hazards. Housekeeping and lighting standards must be maintained.

    Fire & emergency

    High-volume storage creates significant fire load. Sprinkler clearances, emergency exits, and evacuation drills must be current.
