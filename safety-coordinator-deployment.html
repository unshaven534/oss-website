What It Is

Deployment, not advisory

This is a different service model from our OSS Watch / Guard / Overwatch subscriptions — built for a different need.

Safety Coordinator Deployment

Project-based, on-site

You need a person physically present on your project or vessel for a defined scope and duration. We match a Safety Coordinator to your project, and OSS provides compliance oversight of their work throughout the engagement.

OSS Watch / Guard / Overwatch

Ongoing advisory, off-site

A monthly subscription where OSS acts as your outsourced WSHO — inspections, RAs, SWPs, and MOM compliance advisory — without a dedicated person stationed at your site full-time.

Who It's For

Built for contractors with a defined scope

Common fits we see:

Contractors on a fixed-term project

A specific job site or installation that needs MOM-compliant safety coverage for the duration of the contract, without adding permanent headcount.

Marine & offshore project teams

Vessel or terminal-based work requiring a coordinator on-site for the operation window — matched to marine-specific risk exposure.

Clients whose WSH is otherwise covered

Companies with in-house safety management that simply need an extra qualified body deployed to a specific site or shift.

Short-notice or short-duration coverage

Coverage for a period too short or too specific to justify a monthly retainer — a single project, audit window, or peak season.

How It Works

From scope to deployment

A straightforward four-step process — you don't manage recruitment, we handle screening and compliance oversight.

01

Scope the project

We discuss your site, sector, duration, and the coverage you need — full-time, part-time, or shift-based.

02

Match a candidate

We draw from our network of vetted Safety Coordinators and associates, matched to your sector and project risk profile.

03

Deploy to site

The Safety Coordinator is deployed under an OSS service agreement, with agreed scope, hours, and reporting lines confirmed upfront.

04

Ongoing oversight

OSS's WSHO provides compliance oversight for the duration of the deployment — the coordinator isn't left unsupported on-site.

Pricing

Scoped to your project

Deployment pricing depends on duration, sector risk, and coverage hours — so we don't publish a flat rate.

How pricing works

Every deployment is priced against the specific project: duration, site risk profile, and hours of coverage required. Tell us the scope and we'll come back with a straightforward day-rate or project-rate quote.

  • Day-rate or project-rate basis, agreed before deployment starts
  • No minimum retainer commitment — scoped to the project period
  • Rate reflects sector risk (e.g. marine/offshore vs. general site work)
Existing retainer clients (Watch / Guard / Overwatch) receive priority matching and preferred rates on deployment requests.
